Void

June 11th, 2026

A new life appears on this soil Mind free and unseen, devoid of turmoil Instantly the young is shamed "What have you gained?" First drop of essence suppressed on sight. Months and years pass by fast Eyes not comprehending what just passed Thoughts passed on like clockwork A mind the child cannot rework His mind cut in half without being asked. The young man now questions his will It won't matter, it has to only pay the bill The world has placed on him, a vessel for them to fill While he scrambles for his thoughts they think of how to kill The mind rotted with outsiders that's left questioning why it fights uphill? Took the path he's been assigned Charted or not his mind never resigned That he'll escape the bind he knows Holds him in his ripped clothes But his body says its will, too late to be unsigned. While memory starts to fade, final drops of colors spill They land on his quill, they muddied his shaky written will Yet as the clouds of life free the sky, the drops of essence long lost Align themselves before his closing eyes, asking "what have you gained?" Before he turns to dust.
