Fool's Wish

October 4th, 2025

On the wooden rooftop of the village hall No one to call, one step from a free fall The fool stands tall facing away from the wall Unable to crawl, about to lose it all. The haunting bind made by his mind Is rotten, refined, yet he pays no mind Embracing the flame, dirtying his name Taking the shame with no one to name. The gift of life turned on its tail The scale of death, one with no fail With no trail or tale, his body now frail And no one to bail, he's now free to sail. He inhales the pure, fresh air of the rain No pain in his veins as if it was in vain The chain's reign now buried and slain His final exhale keeping him sane.
