Chronostasis

April 28th, 2026

However long has it been since it all started? The way my hands tremble remains unchanged Even though my reflection only showed something uncharted Yet today I'm left wondering if it was fair - everything that was exchanged. Stopped in time, unable to break, who's to say it was right? It can't be helped, so few escapes, only an idiotic sight Coming back and flying away, who's going to answer? I broke peaceful worlds Ripped them one by one Stole everyone's sun Now I'm the one holding the gun Pointing to who? No there's none I'm now wondering who won Too late, it's all said and done. Inside this dim basement I endlessly ran the same route Never knowing what was it all about Is it different this time, or a mere replacement? I can hear it talking about me I can run, I can avoid it Surely it never gets to me If it's all kept under rug I'll never have to fight it What I needed to hear before all of this Was something I buried Thought it was never me A million types of disappointment What do I tell myself in the past? It's funny though, I'm not alone with this pest The "you" still stuck in that basement Is still crying for the future.
