Mortem maledictionis immortalis.

December 20th, 2025

Like an undead rose, unable to wither away All decayed, burnt to ashes, all petals turning gray Even with no way for it to stay It still blooms, unable to be kept at bay. It died twice, thrice, why was there no end? Slipped every time on the same ice, but it would never bend It would blend in, mend itself, even with no sparks to spend. It exploded into nothingness, changing nothing Yet everything No thing remained unturned One thing that slowly burned Burnt out yet it returned.
