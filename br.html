Broken reflection

December 19th, 2025

Keep the leash tight in your hands Then be shocked you're left in sands To be born is to be owned is how it stands In your head, so don't be surprised when a child stands. Keep 'em straight so they don't stray Then watch as their collar starts to decay There's no gray to you if you're the prey When they walk away you'll be left to pray. There's no perfection, that's how your mind copes But how does it cancel destroying your offspring's hopes? You tell them to dream of a nightmare of your own dreams Until they break, your shadow casting over their beams. So waste away, cope in despair Watch the leash fade, call it unfair. Let the anger fill your veins When they refuse to use those chains The ones that haunted their young brains Unable to see their own lanes.
