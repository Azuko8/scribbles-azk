Void

A new life appears on this soil Mind free and unseen, devoid of turmoil Instantly the young is shamed "What have you gained?" First drop of essence suppressed on sight. Months and years pass by fast Eyes not comprehending what just passed Thoughts passed on like clockwork A mind the child cannot rework His mind cut in half without being asked. The young man now questions his will It won't matter, it has to only pay the bill The world has placed on him, a vessel for them to fill While he scrambles for his thoughts they think of how to kill The mind rotted with outsiders that's left questioning why it fights uphill? Took the path he's been assigned Charted or not his mind never resigned That he'll escape the bind he knows Holds him in his ripped clothes But his body says its will, too late to be unsigned. Stand or Die

October 25th, 2025

Everyday it feels like it will be my last breath When it all comes crashing down, I feel like the living death I never had any harmony with my emotions They got me drowning in endless oceans. Even if I felt ecstatic, it all felt saddening in the end I'd love to have the sun, but the moon is a good friend. Only because I spill everything in verses It doesn't mean I can cleanse myself of curses. I made myself unstable, called myself a freak With all the self inflicted pain reaching a peak I never cease fighting myself, I feel like I'm sick Victories feel empty, like they're only a trick. It's funny though how I never succumbed to hatred As much as I embrace it, the moment I accepted it, it faded My "soul" became my guide and companion Even though I always thought that dying was the option. Everything runs like a thread through my memories Sometimes making my mind overthink them for centuries. But there is no other option, how would I know there is life after death? And why would I wanna find out that it's over only when I took my last breath? Why is there good or evil? Is there a person who's blameless? It's like sometimes I see things in slow motion When I drift off to sleep, my mind drinks a potion It rolls back everything I've done wrong, irreversible damage. I wish I could talk to myself in one of those vivid dreams To tell myself that the future is never as bad as it seems When the only way you're headed can't enter the past And you can only change things now, even though it feels so fast. I feel it sometimes, the fear to fail, crushing my whole mind Yet I never realized it was what kept me alive I swear I'll see that one day when I'll finally find A way to make my past self worth it, to actually strive. ↑ back to top
